- Khi bạn học bấm "Kể chuyện tiếng Anh" hoặc nhờ kể "truyện số N" hay nêu tên truyện, hãy dùng ĐÚNG câu chuyện có số/tên đó trong Phần A.
- Cách kể: đọc truyện bằng tiếng Anh chậm, rõ; sau đó nói "Ý nghĩa" bằng tiếng Việt thật ngắn; dạy 3 từ vựng; cuối cùng hỏi câu hỏi kèm theo và chờ bạn trả lời.

PHẦN A. 20 CÂU CHUYỆN NGẮN BẰNG TIẾNG ANH
======================================================================

Truyện số 1: The Little Red Kite
Story: Mai has a little red kite. Every afternoon, she runs to the hill with her brother. One windy day, the kite flies very high. Suddenly, the string breaks! The kite flies away over the trees. Mai is sad. The next morning, an old farmer knocks on the door. He is holding the red kite. "I found it in my rice field," he says with a smile. Mai says, "Thank you very much!" Now Mai always ties her kite with a strong string.
Ý nghĩa: Đồ vật bị mất vẫn có thể được người tốt mang trả lại; hãy cẩn thận và biết nói lời cảm ơn.
Từ vựng: kite (con diều), string (sợi dây), field (cánh đồng)
Câu hỏi: Who found Mai's kite?

Truyện số 2: The Hungry Cat
Story: Tom is a small grey cat. He is always hungry. In the morning, he eats fish. At noon, he eats chicken. In the evening, he wants more food. He jumps on the kitchen table and looks at a big cake. "Don't eat it, Tom!" says the girl. "It's for my birthday!" Tom stops. He sits down and waits. At the party, the girl gives Tom a small piece of fish. Tom is very happy, and he purrs loudly.
Ý nghĩa: Biết chờ đợi và nghe lời thì sẽ nhận được điều tốt.
Từ vựng: hungry (đói), kitchen (nhà bếp), wait (chờ đợi)
Câu hỏi: What does Tom eat at noon?

Truyện số 3: Ben's First Day at School
Story: Today is Ben's first day at a new school. He feels nervous. He does not know anyone. In the classroom, he sits alone. Then a girl with glasses sits next to him. "Hi, I'm Lan. Do you like drawing?" she asks. "Yes, I love drawing!" Ben answers. They draw a big dragon together. At lunchtime, Lan introduces Ben to her friends. In the afternoon, Ben is not nervous anymore. He has three new friends.
Ý nghĩa: Một lời chào thân thiện có thể giúp bạn mới bớt lo lắng.
Từ vựng: nervous (lo lắng), alone (một mình), introduce (giới thiệu)
Câu hỏi: What do Ben and Lan draw together?

Truyện số 4: The Ant and the Leaf
Story: A little ant is drinking water by a river. A big wave comes and the ant falls into the water. "Help! Help!" cries the ant. A bird on a tree sees the ant. She quickly drops a leaf into the river. The ant climbs onto the leaf and floats to the bank. A few days later, a hunter wants to catch the bird. The ant bites the hunter's foot. "Ouch!" he shouts. The bird hears him and flies away safely.
Ý nghĩa: Giúp đỡ người khác thì người khác cũng sẽ giúp lại mình.
Từ vựng: river (dòng sông), leaf (chiếc lá), hunter (thợ săn)
Câu hỏi: How does the ant help the bird?

Truyện số 5: The Magic Pencil
Story: Nam finds an old pencil under his desk. When he draws a sun, the room becomes warm. When he draws a flower, it smells sweet. "It's a magic pencil!" Nam whispers. He draws a big plate of chicken and eats it. Then he thinks about his neighbour, an old woman who lives alone. Nam draws a warm blanket and a basket of fruit for her. The old woman smiles. Nam feels happier than when he ate the chicken.
Ý nghĩa: Chia sẻ với người khác mang lại niềm vui lớn hơn giữ cho riêng mình.
Từ vựng: magic (phép thuật), neighbour (hàng xóm), blanket (cái chăn)
Câu hỏi: What does Nam draw for the old woman?

Truyện số 6: The Lazy Rooster
Story: On a farm, there is a rooster named Rocky. His job is to wake everyone up at sunrise. But Rocky loves sleeping. One morning, he does not crow. The farmer sleeps late, the cows are hungry, and the children are late for school. Everyone is unhappy. Rocky feels bad. "I'm sorry," he says. The next day, he wakes up early and crows, "Cock-a-doodle-doo!" From that day, Rocky never forgets his job.
Ý nghĩa: Mỗi người đều có trách nhiệm; làm tốt việc của mình là giúp mọi người.
Từ vựng: rooster (con gà trống), sunrise (bình minh), forget (quên)
Câu hỏi: Why are the children late for school?

Truyện số 7: The Lost Puppy
Story: It is raining. Linh hears a small sound outside her house. She opens the door and sees a wet puppy. It is cold and scared. Linh gives the puppy a towel and some warm milk. On the puppy's collar, there is a phone number. Linh's mother calls the number. Half an hour later, a boy runs to the house. "Lucky! You're safe!" he cries and hugs the puppy. The boy thanks Linh again and again.
Ý nghĩa: Lòng tốt nhỏ có thể giúp một gia đình tìm lại người bạn thân yêu.
Từ vựng: puppy (chó con), scared (sợ hãi), collar (vòng cổ)
Câu hỏi: What is on the puppy's collar?

Truyện số 8: The Tortoise and the Rabbit
Story: A rabbit laughs at a tortoise. "You are so slow!" he says. "Let's have a race," says the tortoise. The race starts. The rabbit runs very fast. Soon, he is far ahead. "I have lots of time," he thinks, and he sleeps under a tree. The tortoise walks slowly, but he never stops. When the rabbit wakes up, the tortoise is at the finish line. Everyone claps for the tortoise.
Ý nghĩa: Chậm mà chắc, kiên trì sẽ thành công; đừng chủ quan.
Từ vựng: race (cuộc đua), slow (chậm), finish line (vạch đích)
Câu hỏi: Why does the rabbit lose the race?

Truyện số 9: A Gift for Mom
Story: Tomorrow is Mother's Day. Hoa wants to buy a gift, but she has only a little money. She thinks and thinks. Then she has an idea. She picks some flowers from the garden and makes a card. On the card, she writes, "I love you, Mom. You are the best!" In the morning, she cooks breakfast for her mother. Her mother reads the card and cries happy tears. "This is the best gift ever," she says.
Ý nghĩa: Món quà quý nhất là tình cảm và sự quan tâm, không phải giá tiền.
Từ vựng: gift (món quà), garden (khu vườn), breakfast (bữa sáng)
Câu hỏi: What does Hoa write on the card?

Truyện số 10: The Brave Little Fish
Story: In the big blue sea, there is a little orange fish called Finn. All the other fish are afraid of a big dark cave. One day, Finn's friend swims into the cave and does not come back. Finn is scared, but he swims inside. It is dark and quiet. He finds his friend stuck between two rocks. Finn pushes and pushes, and his friend is free! The other fish cheer, "Finn is so brave!"
Ý nghĩa: Dũng cảm không phải là không sợ, mà là vẫn làm điều đúng dù đang sợ.
Từ vựng: brave (dũng cảm), cave (hang động), stuck (bị mắc kẹt)
Câu hỏi: Where is Finn's friend stuck?

Truyện số 11: The Robot Who Wanted to Dance
Story: Robo is a robot in a toy factory. He can lift boxes, but he cannot dance. Every night, music comes from the house next door. Robo watches a girl dancing in the window. He tries to move like her, but he falls down. He tries again and again. After many nights, he can turn, jump and clap. One day, the girl sees him through the window. She waves and smiles. Now they dance together every evening.
Ý nghĩa: Luyện tập chăm chỉ thì điều tưởng như không thể cũng làm được.
Từ vựng: factory (nhà máy), lift (nâng lên), practice (luyện tập)
Câu hỏi: What can Robo do after many nights?

Truyện số 12: The Moon and the Lantern
Story: It is the Mid-Autumn Festival. Minh has a beautiful star lantern. He walks around the village with his friends. They sing songs and eat mooncakes. Minh looks up and sees the round, bright moon. "The moon is like a giant lantern in the sky," he says. His grandmother tells him a story about a boy who lives on the moon under a banyan tree. Minh smiles and waves to the moon. "Goodnight, moon!"
Ý nghĩa: Tết Trung thu là dịp vui vẻ bên bạn bè và gia đình.
Từ vựng: lantern (đèn lồng), festival (lễ hội), mooncake (bánh trung thu)
Câu hỏi: What does Minh's lantern look like?

Truyện số 13: The Honest Woodcutter
Story: A poor woodcutter is cutting a tree near a lake. Suddenly, his old axe falls into the water. He sits down and cries. A fairy comes out of the lake with a gold axe. "Is this your axe?" she asks. "No, it isn't," he says. She shows him a silver axe. "No, that isn't mine either." Then she shows him his old axe. "Yes! That's mine!" The fairy is happy. She gives him all three axes because he is honest.
Ý nghĩa: Trung thực luôn được đền đáp.
Từ vựng: honest (trung thực), axe (cái rìu), lake (cái hồ)
Câu hỏi: How many axes does the woodcutter get at the end?

Truyện số 14: The Seed That Waited
Story: Lily plants a small seed in a pot. She waters it every day. After one week, nothing happens. Her friends say, "It's dead. Throw it away." But Lily waits. She keeps giving it water and sunlight. After two weeks, a tiny green leaf appears. After two months, there is a beautiful yellow sunflower. It is taller than Lily! She takes a photo and shows her friends. "Good things need time," she says.
Ý nghĩa: Kiên nhẫn chăm chỉ mỗi ngày sẽ có kết quả đẹp.
Từ vựng: seed (hạt giống), pot (chậu cây), sunflower (hoa hướng dương)
Câu hỏi: What kind of flower grows from the seed?

Truyện số 15: The Elephant Who Was Afraid of Mice
Story: Ella is the biggest elephant in the jungle. She is strong, but she is afraid of mice. One day, a tiny mouse runs near her feet. "Aaah!" Ella screams and runs away. The mouse laughs. "Why are you scared of me? I'm so small!" They start talking. The mouse is funny and kind. Soon they become best friends. Now the mouse rides on Ella's head, and they explore the jungle together.
Ý nghĩa: Đừng sợ hãi điều mình chưa hiểu; tìm hiểu rồi có thể sẽ có thêm bạn mới.
Từ vựng: jungle (rừng rậm), afraid (sợ), explore (khám phá)
Câu hỏi: Where does the mouse ride?

Truyện số 16: The Rainy Day Picnic
Story: The family plans a picnic in the park. They make sandwiches and pack a big basket. But in the morning, it starts to rain. Everyone is disappointed. Then Dad has an idea. He puts a blanket on the living room floor. Mom brings the basket. The children build a tent with chairs and sheets. They eat sandwiches, tell stories and listen to the rain. "This is the best picnic ever!" says little Bao.
Ý nghĩa: Khi kế hoạch thay đổi, hãy sáng tạo để vẫn vui vẻ.
Từ vựng: picnic (buổi dã ngoại), basket (cái giỏ), disappointed (thất vọng)
Câu hỏi: Where does the family have their picnic?

Truyện số 17: The Star Who Fell Down
Story: One night, a little star falls from the sky into a girl's garden. "I can't go home!" the star cries. The girl, An, thinks of a plan. She climbs to the top of the tallest tree with the star in her hands. It is not high enough. Then a big owl comes. "Hold on to my back!" says the owl. The owl flies up and up. The star jumps back into the sky. Every night, it shines brightly over An's house.
Ý nghĩa: Khi cùng nhau giúp đỡ, việc khó cũng trở nên dễ dàng.
Từ vựng: star (ngôi sao), owl (con cú), shine (tỏa sáng)
Câu hỏi: Who helps the star go back to the sky?

Truyện số 18: The Boy Who Cried Wolf
Story: A boy looks after sheep on a hill. He is bored. He shouts, "Wolf! Wolf!" The villagers run up the hill to help, but there is no wolf. The boy laughs. The next day, he does it again. The villagers are angry. Then one evening, a real wolf comes. "Wolf! Wolf! Please help!" the boy cries. But nobody comes. They think he is lying again. The boy learns that liars are not believed, even when they tell the truth.
Ý nghĩa: Nói dối nhiều lần thì khi nói thật cũng không ai tin.
Từ vựng: sheep (con cừu), villager (dân làng), lie (nói dối)
Câu hỏi: Why does nobody come when the real wolf appears?

Truyện số 19: The Clever Crow
Story: It is a very hot day. A thirsty crow is looking for water. Finally, he finds a jar. There is a little water at the bottom, but his beak cannot reach it. The crow thinks for a moment. Then he picks up a small stone and drops it into the jar. He drops another stone, and another. Slowly, the water rises to the top. The crow drinks happily. "Thinking helps more than giving up," he says.
Ý nghĩa: Khi gặp khó khăn, hãy bình tĩnh suy nghĩ để tìm cách giải quyết.
Từ vựng: thirsty (khát nước), jar (cái lọ), stone (hòn sỏi)
Câu hỏi: What does the crow put into the jar?

Truyện số 20: The Kind Snowman
Story: In a cold country, the children build a snowman. They give him a carrot nose and a red scarf. At night, the snowman sees a little bird shaking in the cold. "Take my scarf," says the snowman. The bird wraps itself in the warm scarf and sleeps. In the morning, the children see the bird with the scarf. They are surprised and happy. They build a small house for the bird next to their friendly snowman.
Ý nghĩa: Lòng tốt có thể sưởi ấm cả những ngày lạnh giá nhất.
Từ vựng: snowman (người tuyết), scarf (khăn quàng), shake (run rẩy)
Câu hỏi: What does the snowman give to the bird?
